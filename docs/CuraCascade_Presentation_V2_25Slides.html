03 // BIOMEDICAL FOUNDATIONS
Biomedical Foundations I: DNA, Genes, Variants & Proteins
Understanding how genetic variation causes human disease from first principles.
DNA & Chromosomes
• DNA is a chemical double-helix encoded by four chemical bases: A, C, G, and T.
• Human cells package DNA into 23 pairs of chromosomes (46 total).
• Think of chromosomes as large reference volumes containing recipes for life.
Genes & Proteins
• A gene is a specific sequence of DNA bases providing instructions to build a protein.
• Humans have ~20,000 genes.
• Proteins perform cellular work: building muscle (MYH7), conducting heart currents (SCN5A), or repairing DNA (BRCA1).
Genetic Variants
• A variant is a typographical change in the DNA sequence (a typo in the recipe).
• Benign Variants: Harmless natural diversity found in healthy individuals.
• Pathogenic Variants: Damaging alterations that disrupt protein function, causing disease.
Allele Frequency & MCAF
• Allele Frequency (AF): Percentage of people carrying a variant in healthy reference cohorts (gnomAD).
• Whiffin et al. Framework: If a condition occurs in 1 in 10,000 people, a variant present in 1% of healthy adults CANNOT be causal.
• Establishes MCAF thresholds.

04 // BIOMEDICAL FOUNDATIONS
Biomedical Foundations II: Gene Validity vs. Variant Pathogenicity
Crucial distinction: evaluating whether a gene causes disease vs. whether a mutation breaks a gene.
1. GENE-LEVEL CLINICAL VALIDITY (CLINGEN)
Evaluates the fundamental biological relationship: Does variation in Gene X cause Disease Y?
• Definitive: Overwhelming human genetic & experimental replication.
• Strong: Substantial genetic evidence; pending additional families.
• Moderate: Preliminary evidence; biologically plausible.
• Limited: Weak evidence; 1–2 isolated case reports.
• Disputed: Conflicting evidence; subsequent studies fail to reproduce link.
• Refuted: Disproven! Robust case-control studies rule out causality.
2. VARIANT-LEVEL PATHOGENICITY (CLINVAR / ACMG)
Evaluates a specific typographical change in a gene: Does this particular mutation impair protein function?
• Pathogenic (P): Proven causal mutation altering critical protein domains.
• Likely Pathogenic (LP): >90% certainty of being disease-causing.
• Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS): Unknown clinical effect.
• Likely Benign (LB): >90% probability of being harmless neutral variation.
• Benign (B): Proven harmless; occurs frequently in healthy populations.
THE LOGICAL DEPENDENCY TRAP: Variant pathogenicity is conditionally predicated on gene validity. If a gene is Disputed or Refuted for a disease, NO variant within that gene can legitimately be classified as Pathogenic for that disease!

06 // ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS
Why Clinical Evidence Disagrees: Four Structural Barriers
Analyzing the semantic, organizational, and temporal reasons discrepancies persist.
BARRIER 1
Granularity Mismatch
ClinGen evaluates the gene entity (G). ClinVar tracks the variant entity (V). PanelApp tracks panels (P).

Without a relational bridge connecting variant records to gene validity determinations, updates at the gene level remain invisible to variant pipelines.
BARRIER 2
Ontological Divergence
A disease in ClinGen may be recorded as 'Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy 1' (MONDO:0005045), while ClinVar labels variants as 'Familial hypertrophic cardiomyopathy' (MedGen:C0007194) or 'Cardiovascular phenotype'.

Direct string matching fails across >60% of entries.
BARRIER 3
Asynchronous Cadence
ClinGen expert panels meet periodically to review specific domains (e.g. Arrhythmia in 2021, Cardiomyopathy in 2024).

Commercial diagnostic labs lack staff to manually re-curate historical variant submissions, leaving stale assertions active for years.
BARRIER 4
Missing Assertions
Many downgraded genes lack variant submissions in public archives because commercial labs treat internal testing data as proprietary.

In our benchmark audit, 47% of false negatives occurred simply because the local database snapshot contained zero variant records.

12 // RELATIONAL SCHEMA
Relational Schema & Entity-Relationship Architecture (3NF)
Third Normal Form normalized relational structure linking upstream curation with downstream assertions.
3NF RELATIONAL SPECIFICATIONS
• Third Normal Form (3NF) Elimination of Anomalies: Non-key attributes depend strictly on primary keys. Eliminates insertion, update, and deletion anomalies across genes, diseases, curations, and variant records.

• Referential Integrity & Cascading Constraints: Strict Foreign Key enforcement: deleting a gene or disease cascades predictably without creating orphaned variant records. Enforced via SQLite PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON.

• Compound Indexing for Join Acceleration: B-tree composite indexes on (gene_symbol, disease_id) and (gene_symbol, variation_id) optimize join resolution, reducing query plan scan cost from O(N) to O(log N).

• Dual Scale Modes: Showcase Mode (36 genes, 34 findings) vs. Full Corpus Mode (3,033 genes, 51,025 variants, 51,251 findings).

14 // MATCHING LOGIC
Disease-Aware Ontology Matching & Crosswalk Heuristics
Preventing catastrophic false-positive alerts on pleiotropic multi-disease genes.
THE FATAL FLAW OF GENE-SYMBOL-ONLY MATCHING
In human genetics, a single gene frequently causes multiple completely distinct diseases (pleiotropy).

Consider ACTN2 (Actinin Alpha 2):
• Disease A: Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM) → ClinGen: DISPUTED.
• Disease B: Distal Myopathy (Muscle weakness) → ClinGen: DEFINITIVE.

If an algorithm matches solely by gene symbol 'ACTN2', it sees a Disputed evaluation and falsely invalidates all variants for Distal Myopathy! This would deprive muscular dystrophy patients of life-saving molecular diagnoses.

CuraCascade Solution: The unit of analysis is strictly the Gene–Disease Dyad (G, D), never the gene alone.
CURACASCADE 3-TIER MATCHING HIERARCHY
• TIER 1: Exact Ontology Concept Crosswalk: Direct mapping between ClinGen MONDO identifier and ClinVar MedGen / OMIM concept ID via curated crosswalk dictionary (ontology_crosswalk.py). Match Confidence = HIGH.

• TIER 2: Substring Matching with Keyword Exclusion Guards: When concept IDs are absent, performs normalized string matching but enforces strict negative keyword guards (e.g. 'hypertrophic cardiomyopathy' does NOT match 'distal myopathy'; 'Brugada' does NOT match 'Long QT'). Match Confidence = MEDIUM.

• TIER 3: Fallback & Review Required: Unmapped or generalized disease terms (e.g. 'Cardiovascular phenotype') are marked as REVIEW_REQUIRED and excluded from automatic discrepancy flagging to protect precision.
